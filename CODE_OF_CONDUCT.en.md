# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

We encourage an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative community. This Code of Conduct describes our expectations for participant behavior and the consequences for unacceptable behavior.

## 2. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all contributions to the repository (issues, pull requests, comments, project-related emails, discussions on the project page, and any public communication related to it).

## 3. Our standards

Participants are expected to behave respectfully and professionally. Among other things, we expect:

- Treat everyone with courtesy and respect.
- Accept constructive criticism with an open mind.
- Improve clarity of communications when there is confusion.
- Seek cooperative solutions instead of attacking others.

## 4. Expected behavior

Examples of behavior we encourage include, but are not limited to:

- Use inclusive and respectful language.
- Be considerate of others and their time.
- Embrace diversity of opinions, experiences, and styles.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.

## 5. Unacceptable behavior

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- Personal attacks, insults, and offensive language.
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age, physical appearance, nationality, or any other protected characteristic.
- Threats, harassment, or intimidation.
- Posting private or confidential information without permission.
- Attributing malicious intent without evidence.

## 6. Enforcement and consequences

Project maintainers reserve the right to take action against any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct. Actions may include (but are not limited to):

- Formal warnings.
- Removal of comments or disruptive material.
- Temporary or permanent blocking from contributing or community spaces.
- Removal from maintainer roles.

## 7. How to report an issue

If you witness or are the target of behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it as follows:

1. Open an issue (private if possible) titled "Code of Conduct report" and describe:
   - What happened.
   - Where and when it happened (link to issue/PR/chat if applicable).
   - People involved (if known).
   - Relevant evidence or messages.

2. If you prefer privacy or are uncomfortable making a public report, contact the repository maintainer via their profile: https://github.com/Luiscrespo-bot

Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and respond as soon as possible.

## 8. Legal scope and additional details

This Code of Conduct does not replace local laws or broader organizational policies. Maintainers will act in accordance with applicable law.

## 9. Attribution
